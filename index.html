Integrated Financial Model & Corporate Valuation Simulation

Role: Financial Modeler / FP&A Analyst (Project)  ·  Focus: Corporate Finance, Valuation, and Strategic Planning

A self-directed project designed to translate complex business operations into actionable financial projections, bridging core accounting principles with strategic corporate finance to demonstrate end-to-end capabilities in valuation, forecasting, and data-backed management decisions.

Key deliverables: a fully linked 3-statement model (Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow) driven by robust operating assumptions; corporate valuation via DCF, WACC, and Comparable Company Analysis; an FP&A framework for annual budgeting with budget-vs-actuals variance analysis; and risk/scenario planning through sensitivity analysis and stress-testing.

Technical stack: professional-grade Advanced Excel — SUMIFS and nested IF logic, dynamic mixed cell references, and conditional formatting — built in alignment with CFA and FMVA financial modeling standards.
